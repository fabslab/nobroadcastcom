About

No Broadcast, a dynamic powerhouse of sound that combines elements of post-rock and alternative rock to create an unforgettable musical experience. At its core, No Broadcast is a three-piece rock band hailing from the South Island of New Zealand, established in 2007. Drawing inspiration from esteemed New Zealand acts like HDU and Jakob, as well as international sensations like Mogwai, Radiohead, Sigur Ros, and The Veils, No Broadcast's music resonates with a captivating energy.

With a decade-long journey, No Broadcast has delighted audiences across the country, captivating them with their instinctive ability to let their music evolve organically. Their live performances are nothing short of exceptional, leaving a lasting impression on anyone fortunate enough to witness them. The band has graced and shared the stage with a diverse array of local and international artists, including Beast Wars, Die! Die! Die!, Bauhaus, Skinny HOBOs, Clap Clap Riot, Mountaineater (featuring former HDU members), An Emerald City, Jakob, Dead Favours, and Yawning Man.

No Broadcast is the brainchild of songwriter Josh Braden, whose vision and soul drive the band's artistic direction. He has self-produced all of the group's albums to date. However, No Broadcast has evolved into a collective experience, incorporating numerous musicians who have contributed to their albums and joined them on tour.

The most recent release, Album The Common Thread, found its way through the airwaves to the number one spot on local radio networks & NO.7 in the New Zealand Album charts. The album also garnered acclaim from a number of music press and online media outlets.
